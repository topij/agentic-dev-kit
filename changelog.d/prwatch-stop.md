## pr-watch.md: the converged-head review request is stated in `--json` fields

CHANGED: the *Stop conditions* bullet used to tell you to request a review whenever `review_bots.coverage` showed the head uncovered, even during a trusted check-borne outage, where the engine deliberately prints `⚠ review unavailable` and no `⚠ review owed`. It now states the engine's own predicate in the fields the `--json` poll carries: request at the converged head when `review_bots.signal` is `ok` and a configured bot has no head-covering `coverage` entry, no `comment_verdicts` entry, no trusted blocking `pending` entry, and no trusted check-surface `unavailable` entry. `⚠ review owed` is the text render of that condition; the JSON has no field for it. When `signal` is not `ok`, re-poll; on a trusted check-surface outage, take the fallback panel. The *Loop* step for `converged` now points at the same condition. No action is needed unless you copied the old bullet into your own workflow docs or automation, in which case key it on those fields; the engine is unchanged.
